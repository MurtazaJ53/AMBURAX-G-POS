# AMBURAX POS — Brutal Truth

## The scope is large
125 workspaces is a serious SaaS platform, not a small POS.

## ERPNext is both advantage and trap
It saves years of ERP/accounting work. Duplicating its rules creates conflicting truths.

## Commerce is harder than publishing
The hard problems are reservations, overselling, order lifecycle, fulfillment, returns, refunds, API limits, credential expiry and reconciliation.

## Microservices can destroy velocity
Thirty-plus independently deployed services too early can make a small team slower than a modular monolith. Define boundaries now; extract with evidence.

## Offline POS is a separate engineering project
Conflict resolution, payment behavior, sequencing and reconciliation must be designed and tested before promising full offline.

## AI before data quality is theater
Bad catalog/stock/customer data creates bad automation.

## Integrations are permanent maintenance
APIs, scopes, policies, rate limits and webhooks change.

## The product should be sold as
One system to run the merchant's physical store, online business, inventory, customers, orders, Khata, B2B and intelligence.

## The quality bar
Reliable five-channel infrastructure is better than twenty unreliable connectors.
